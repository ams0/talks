## Lines to say verbatim

- Slide 3: "Hands up if you used an AI tool this week. Keep it up if something AI does in your business every week without you touching it. Keep it up if you can tell me the euros it earned or saved you last month. That drop is what we close in the next forty minutes."
- Slide 4: "Same street, same supplier, same tomatoes. One restaurant has a queue. The ingredients don't explain that. The kitchen does."
- Slide 8: "I talk for seventeen minutes. You work for twenty. Find your partner now."
- Slide 10: "Automate to the extreme. Decide like an owner. Review like a scientist."
- Slide 22: "NEEDS HUMAN is your control point, in one line of text."
- Slide 25: "Next time you look at a competitor, don't ask which AI they use. Ask what they refused to hand over — and how fast they change their mind."

## What goes wrong in the room

- **Processes that are too big.** "Marketing" can't be X-rayed in three minutes. Push to one repeating task.
- **Everything is "needs judgment".** Ask: "Would a sharp intern with a checklist get this 80% right?"
- **Confidential data pasted into consumer chat apps.** Say the warning on slide 21 out loud before starting the timer.
- **No Wi-Fi or no assistant on the phone.** Pair those people with someone who has one, or run your backup on screen and let the room critique it.
- **Energy dip after Exercise 1.** Keep the slide 20 explanation to 60 seconds; the template is on the handout.
